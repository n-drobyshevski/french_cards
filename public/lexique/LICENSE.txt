fr-lemmas.json is derived from Lexique 3.83 (Boris New & Christophe Pallier, http://www.lexique.org),
licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), with a small curated list
of slang lemmas added. Format: [lemma, part of speech, gender, frequency per million words], sorted by the
accent-folded lemma. Rebuild with: node scripts/build-lexique.mjs

fr-conj.json (verb tables for «Контекст» → «Спряжение») is derived from the same Lexique 3.83 data under
the same licence: the verbs the conjugation rules in lib/core/conjugate.ts get wrong, as présent, imparfait,
futur and past participle. Rebuild with: node scripts/build-conjugation.mjs

fr-morpho.json («Контекст» → «Слово по частям») is derived from Lexique 3.83's morphology column under the
same licence: [lemma, "prefix-|root|-suffix", frequency]. Rebuild with: node scripts/build-morpho.mjs

fr-cefr.json («Уровень языка», an estimated CEFR level per word) is derived from FLELex, a separate resource
under its own licence, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/); it is kept in
its own file and never merged into the Lexique files above. Source: https://cental.uclouvain.be/cefrlex/flelex/
- François, T., Gala, N., Watrin, P. & Fairon, C. (2014). FLELex: a graded lexical resource for French
  foreign learners. Proceedings of LREC 2014, 3766–3773.
- Pintard, A. & François, T. (2020). Combining Expert Knowledge with Frequency Information to Infer CEFR
  Levels for Words. Proceedings of READI 2020, 85–92 (the «level» column of FLELex TreeTagger-Beacco).
Format: { v: 1, beacco: [6 strings], crf: [6 strings] }, string i holding the keys of level A1…C2 joined
with "|"; beacco is the TreeTagger-Beacco level per lemma (key~pos where it depends on the part of speech),
crf the multi-word expressions of FLELex CRF at the first level where they occur. Rebuild with:
node scripts/build-cefr.mjs <directory with Beacco.txt and CRF.txt>
